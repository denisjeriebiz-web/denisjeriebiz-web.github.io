PPC Fachman – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

PPC Fachman is a private internal tool operated by Denis Jerie (denis.jerie.biz@gmail.com). It is used only by its operator to manage Google Ads accounts of his clients.

What Google user data we access

  • Google Ads data (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords): account structure, campaigns, ad groups, keywords, ads, assets, budgets, bids and performance reports of the Google Ads accounts the signed-in user has access to.
  • Google Analytics data (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly): read-only aggregated reports of GA4 properties the signed-in user has access to.

We do not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How we use it

The data is used only to analyse advertising performance and, after explicit human approval, to apply changes to the Google Ads accounts on behalf of the account owner. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or training of AI models, and we do not sell it.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and downloaded reports are stored only locally on the operator's computer. Data is not transferred to third parties, except that report extracts may be processed by the operator's AI assistant during analysis. Data of a client is shared only with that client.

Limited Use

PPC Fachman's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have any stored data deleted, email denis.jerie.biz@gmail.com.
